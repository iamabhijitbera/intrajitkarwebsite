About Debanshu Interior Decoration

Debanshu Interior Decoration is an interior decoration and design practice based in Howrah, West Bengal. We work with homeowners, business owners and property developers to plan, design and execute interiors for apartments, independent houses, offices, shops and commercial premises across Howrah and its surrounding areas.

Our Design Philosophy

Good interiors are not about filling a room. They are about proportion, light, storage and material honesty. We design spaces that look considered on day one and still work comfortably years later, avoiding trends that date quickly and choosing finishes that hold up to everyday Indian family life.

Our Approach

Every project begins with a site visit and a conversation about how you actually use your space. From there we develop layouts, material palettes and lighting plans, agree the scope with you, and then execute with our own supervision on site so the drawings and the finished result stay aligned.

Residential Interiors

For homes we cover complete interiors — modular kitchens, wardrobes, TV and feature walls, false ceilings, lighting, pooja spaces, study corners and custom furniture — or single rooms if you prefer to work in phases.

Commercial Interiors

For offices, shops, showrooms and studios we plan interiors around footfall, staff workflow, display and durability, using finishes selected for heavy daily use and lighting designed to present your space and products well.

Attention to Detail

Edges, joints, shutter gaps, handle alignment and light temperature are the details that separate an average interior from a premium one. We check them, and we do not consider a project finished until the finishing is right.
